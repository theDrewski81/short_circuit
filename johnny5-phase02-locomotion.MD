Phase 02 (Locomotion) of the Johnny 5 robot. Work on branch `sim/chassis-env` under `simulation/chassis/`.

Key modelling decisions for the chassis MuJoCo sim (built Session, ~2026-06-22):
- **Treads modelled as four driven wheels** (front + rear per side spanning the 120 mm wheelbase), NOT a deformable belt. A single wheel per side collapses the fore-aft contact patch and the tall robot nose-dives; the four-wheel layout restores the support polygon. All four are driven in left/right pairs (the real belt couples idler to sprocket).
- **DC motor** = torque actuator, `gear = stall/2` per wheel, joint `damping = stall/(2·no_load)` so the per-side torque-speed line matches one brushed motor. Stall 0.25 N·m, no-load 12.9 rad/s (Pololu 150:1 N20 scaled 12 V→7.4 V) — confirm on bench.
- Geometry locked to `mechanical/freecad/params.csv` via `params.py` (`J5_PARAMS_CSV` env var overrides path). MJCF generated by `build_mjcf.py` → `johnny5_chassis.xml` (don't hand-edit).
- Measured envelope: forward ~0.30 m/s, in-place yaw ~0.34 rad/s (skid-steer resistance-limited). Env command caps V_MAX 0.28, W_MAX 0.30.
- Env `JohnnyChassisEnv` (env.py): command-conditioned PPO target, 50 Hz, 9-d obs, 2-d action, reward = vel-track + yaw-track − energy − stability − lateral-skid. Stable under random policy (no NaN/tip).

Training (Task 4): SB3 PPO scaffold in `train.py` + `config.yaml` (2×64 MLP), ONNX export in `export_onnx.py` → `policies/locomotion_v1.onnx`. Real training runs on the home lab (see [[sandbox-no-torch]]). Torch-free `cem_smoke.py` validated env-learnability + onnxruntime deploy path in-sandbox.
